Who I Am

Bridging cybersecurity expertise with digital forensics and proactive threat detection.

I am a Cyber Security postgraduate student pursuing an M.Sc. in Cyber Security at Amity University Rajasthan, with a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Forensic Science from Medicaps University.

My interests span Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT), Web Application Security, Network Security, Digital Forensics, and Security Operations. I have practical exposure to tools such as Burp Suite, OWASP ZAP, Nmap, Wireshark, FTK, Autopsy, and Belkasoft.

I enjoy investigating security weaknesses, analyzing technical evidence, and translating findings into clear, actionable reports. With a strong foundation in both forensic science and cybersecurity, I aim to contribute to identifying vulnerabilities, strengthening security defenses, and supporting digital investigations.

My goal is to turn curiosity into meaningful security insights and help build safer, more resilient digital systems.
